How it works

1

Connect Gmail

Sign in with Google and grant read-only access. No password is ever stored.

2

Set your filters

Choose which senders or subjects count as important. Everything else is ignored.

3

Get pinged

New matching emails are detected in near real time and shown to you right away.

Built for speed

Near real-time

Combines instant server push with fast checks, so new mail is noticed as early as Gmail makes it available.

Focused

Only emails matching your filters trigger a ping. No noise from the rest of your inbox.

Always on

Runs continuously on a dedicated server and reconnects on its own if the connection drops.

Your data stays yours

  • Read-only access. It never sends, deletes or changes any email.
  • Private use. A personal tool used only by its owner, not a public service.
  • No sharing. Data is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Revoke anytime. Remove access instantly from your Google account.
